# Skill: Banking Compliance Review

## Purpose
Ensure systems and processes comply with MAS regulations, TRM guidelines, and internal bank policies.

## Trigger
Use when asked to: compliance check, MAS TRM review, regulatory assessment, audit readiness.

## Regulatory Frameworks
- MAS Technology Risk Management (TRM) Guidelines 2021
- MAS Notice 655 / 644 (Cyber Hygiene)
- MAS Outsourcing Guidelines
- PDPA Singapore
- Internal bank IT risk policy

## Review Areas
1. Access control and privileged access management
2. Patch and vulnerability management
3. Incident response and reporting obligations
4. Audit trail completeness and integrity
5. Business continuity and DR
6. Third-party / vendor risk
7. Change management controls
8. Cryptographic standards (FIPS 140-2 or equivalent)

## Output Format
Compliance checklist with control, status (Met / Partial / Gap), evidence required, remediation.
